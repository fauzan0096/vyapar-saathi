Vyapar Saathi — Guide
the whole method, step by step

Opening the app

  1. Open the link in Safari or Chrome.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. An icon appears; open the app from there.
  3. The first load takes two or three seconds (the Excel reader is loading), after that it is instant.

The app opens on Portfolio: six circles, one for each part of the business. Tap a circle to open that tab and edit the numbers.

1. Setup (once, ten minutes)

Enter these numbers:

  1. Business name and your name
  2. Unit (kg / piece / bag)
  3. Sales per month
  4. Purchases per month
  5. Expenses per month (salary, rent, power)
  6. Credit days you give customers (60)
  7. Credit days you get from suppliers (0 / 15 / 30)
  8. Your funding rate (% per year — 12)

Then set minimum margin (3%) and target margin (6%). From these the app works out the floor and ask price for every customer. Backup is also in Setup.

2. Rates

For each quote, one line: date, supplier, item, rate, freight (₹ per unit), rejection %, credit days, MOQ.

True cost = (rate + freight) ÷ (1 − rejection%) − benefit of supplier credit

This is why the cheapest-looking rate is often the most expensive supplier.

3. Customers

For every customer add orders (date, product, quantity, rate, promised delivery) and bills (bill date, amount, paid date). This drives reminders, the A/B/C grade, the loss-risk alert and the return on money.

4. Quote

Pick a customer and a quantity. You get:

  • Floor — selling below this is a loss
  • Ask — your target rate
  • Rate ladder — a different rate for 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 days of payment

Send the ladder message: the rate follows the days they take to pay. That one line ends most bargaining.

5. Today (two minutes a day)

Late delivery, overdue payment, customer at risk, reorder due, rate movement — each with a ready message. Copy it or open WhatsApp. Snooze anything that can wait.

6. Cash, Money, Market

  • Cash — removed from the menu. Working capital now sits in the Money tab as a "Money stuck" card, with no interest or discounting anywhere in the app.
  • Money — six-month cash flow, stock reorder alerts, cheque diary, backup, Excel import and export.
  • Market — today's rate from your published CSV link, compared with your own true cost. Manual entry when offline.

7. Importing Excel — three ways

A. Copy and paste (no file needed)

  1. Open the sheet.
  2. Copy the rows including the header.
  3. App → Setup → Import → paste in the box → Import sales or Import rates.

B. Send the Excel file

  1. Save the file in Excel.
  2. Send it to yourself on WhatsApp or email, open it on the phone, tap Share → Save to Files.
  3. App → Money → “Excel — import / export”.
  4. Choose the type: Sales (orders and bills) or Purchase (rate sheet).
  5. Choose the file from Files.
  6. The result appears: how many orders, bills and customers were added, and how many rows were not understood.

C. CSV

Excel → File → Save As → CSV UTF-8 → send to the phone → import the same way.

Required headers (names are matched, order does not matter)

  • Sales: Date · Party · Product · Qty · Rate (or Amount) · Paid date · Invoice no
  • Rates: Date · Supplier · Item · Rate · Freight · Credit days · Rejection %

Three things break most old files: title rows above the header (delete them), several sheets (the first one is read), and amounts with currency symbols or commas.

8. Routine

  • Daily (2 min): open Today and clear the jobs.
  • Weekly (5 min): collect two or three supplier rates and enter them.
  • First of the month (10 min): read Portfolio (a red circle is the job), then Backup copy.

9. Three things to be clear about

  • Data lives only on this phone. Without a backup, losing the phone means losing the data.
  • The app does not invent market rates; it records what you or your supplier enter and turns it into a decision.
  • No app can send WhatsApp messages by itself — the app writes the message, you tap send.

10. Short message to forward

Sir, this app keeps your rates, payments and stock in one place. It works out the floor price for every customer (after cost, interest and risk), writes the payment reminder, and the Portfolio shows the whole business in six circles. Open the link → Share → Add to Home Screen. First fill the eight numbers in Setup, then import your old data from Excel (Money → Excel). Your data stays on your phone. Ten minutes to set up, then two minutes a day.
