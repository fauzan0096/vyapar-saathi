Vyapar Saathi — Guide
the whole method, step by step

1. Install (one minute)

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. The icon then opens full screen and works offline. No store, no download, no login.

2. Five tabs — what is in each

  • Portfolio — six circles: cash received, overdue, stock, customer grade, margin, cash cycle. A red circle is this week’s job.
  • Today — two big buttons (add a rate, add a bill) and today’s jobs with ready messages.
  • Customers — every customer with money outstanding; open one for orders, bills, grade and statement.
  • Money — customer statement, Excel import/export, cash flow, money stuck, rejection log, backup. Quote, Rates and Market are under More.
  • Setup — the eight numbers, margins, products, sample data and backup.

Every tab also shows a one-line header at the top saying what it holds.

3. Setup (once, ten minutes)

  1. Business name and your name, unit (kg / piece / bag)
  2. Sales per month, purchases per month, expenses per month
  3. Credit days you give (60) and credit days you get (0 / 15 / 30)
  4. Stock days you hold
  5. Minimum margin (3%) and target margin (6%)

Leave the funding rate at 0 — there is no interest anywhere in the app. From these numbers the app works out the floor and ask price for every customer.

4. Add a rate and add a bill (twenty seconds each)

Add rate (on Today): supplier, item, rate, credit days. Freight and rejection are optional.

Add bill (on Today): customer, amount, bill date. Mark it paid the day the money arrives.

Everything else in the app is calculated from these two entries.

5. Today and the reminder bar

  • The bar at the top says what needs a reminder: overdue money, late delivery, items to order.
  • Tomorrow or Next week puts a payment follow-up into the phone calendar (an .ics file: open it in Files to add the event).
  • Each job on Today has a ready message — copy it or open WhatsApp.

6. Customers and the statement

Open a customer for orders, bills, grade and statement. Print / Save as PDF makes a clean one-page statement: bills, due dates, ageing, delivery % and rejection % — send it on WhatsApp.

7. Money tab

  • Statement — pick a customer and open it.
  • Excel import/export — .xlsx or .csv in, orders + bills or rate sheet out.
  • Cash flow — six months of sales, bills raised and cash received.
  • Money stuck — to receive, in stock, to pay, net blocked. No interest, no discounting.
  • Rejection log — mark rejected quantity per order; the last 90 days’ percentage on top.
  • Backup — copy, download, restore; the app keeps a daily snapshot of the last three days.

8. Importing Excel — three ways

  1. Copy and paste: copy the rows with the header, paste them in Setup → Import.
  2. File: send the .xlsx to yourself on WhatsApp, tap Share → Save to Files, then Money → Excel → pick the file. The first sheet is read.
  3. CSV: Excel → Save As → CSV UTF-8, then the same steps.

Headers: Sales — Date, Party, Product, Qty, Rate (or Amount), Paid date, Invoice no. Rates — Date, Supplier, Item, Rate, Freight, Credit days, Rejection %.

Three things break most old files: title rows above the header, several sheets, and amounts with currency symbols or commas.

9. Routine

  • Daily (2 min): open Today and clear the jobs.
  • Weekly (5 min): collect two or three supplier rates.
  • First of the month (10 min): read Portfolio (a red circle is the job), then Backup copy.

10. Four things to be clear about

  • Data lives only on this phone. Without a backup, losing the phone means losing the data.
  • The app does not invent market rates; it records what you or your supplier enter and turns it into a decision.
  • No app can send WhatsApp messages by itself — the app writes the message, you tap send.
  • There is no interest, no discounting and no lending in the app.

11. Short message to forward

Sir, this app keeps your rates, payments and stock in one place. It works out the floor price for every customer (after cost and risk), writes the payment reminder, and the Portfolio shows the whole business in six circles. Open the link → Share → Add to Home Screen. Fill the eight numbers in Setup, then add a rate and a bill on Today. Your data stays on your phone. Ten minutes to set up, then two minutes a day.
