Vyapar Saathi — Guide
poora tareeqa, step by step

App kholna

  1. Link kholein (Safari/Chrome me).
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen — icon ban jaayega, phir home screen se seedha khulega.
  3. Pehli baar 2-3 second lag sakte hain (Excel padhne wali library load hoti hai), uske baad turant.

App khulte hi Portfolio khulta hai — 6 circle. Har circle ek hissa batata hai; circle dabaayein to wahi tab khul jaata hai.

1. Setup (ek baar, 10 minute)

Ye 8 number bharein:

  1. Business ka naam, aapka naam
  2. Unit (kg / piece / bag)
  3. Mahine ki bikri (₹)
  4. Mahine ki kharid (₹)
  5. Mahine ka kharcha (salary, rent, bijli)
  6. Customer ko kitne din ka credit (60 din)
  7. Supplier se kitne din ka credit (0 / 15 / 30)
  8. Paisa kitne % par milta hai (OD/CC — 12%)

Phir minimum margin (3%) aur target margin (6%) — inhi se app har customer ka floor rate aur ask rate nikaalta hai. Setup me hi Backup hai.

2. Rates tab

Jo rate mile, ek line me: Date, Supplier, Item, Rate, Freight (₹/unit), Rejection %, Credit days, MOQ.

App khud True column nikaalta hai:
True cost = (rate + freight) ÷ (1 − rejection%) − supplier credit ka faayda

Matlab: jo rate list me sabse sasta dikh raha hai, wo asli me sabse mehnga ho sakta hai.

3. Customers tab

Har customer ke liye orders (date, product, qty, rate, promised delivery) aur bills (bill date, amount, paid date) daalein. Isi se reminder, grade A/B/C, churn ka alarm aur “paisa ka return” nikalta hai.

4. Quote tab

Customer + quantity chunein. App dikhata hai:

  • Floor — isse neeche bechna = ghata
  • Ask — target rate
  • Rate ladder — 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 din payment par alag rate

Ladder ka message seedha bhej dein: “jitne din me payment, utna rate.”

5. Today tab (roz 2 minute)

Delivery late, payment overdue, customer jaane ka khatra, order ka time, rate ka badlaav — har kaam par ready message. Copy karein ya WhatsApp me kholein; jo abhi nahi karna snooze kar dein.

6. Cash, Money, Market

  • Cash — working capital, credit ki asli lagat, TReDS, early payment ka faayda.
  • Money — 6 mahine ka cash flow, stock ka “order karein” alert, cheque diary, backup, Excel import-export.
  • Market — aaj ka market rate (Google Sheet ka published CSV link), aur aapke supplier rate se fark. Internet na ho to manual rate.

7. Excel se data laana — 3 tareeqe

A. Sabse aasan: Copy–Paste (file ki zaroorat nahi)

  1. Excel me data kholo.
  2. Header ke saath rows select karo → Copy.
  3. App → Setup → Import → box me paste → “Import sales” ya “Import rates”.

B. Excel file seedha (.xlsx)

  1. Excel me file save karo.
  2. File phone par le aao: WhatsApp/Email se apne aap ko bhejo → file par tap → Share → Save to Files.
  3. App → Money → “Excel — data laayein / le jaayein”.
  4. “1. Kaisa data hai?” me chuno: Sales (orders + bills) ya Purchase (rate sheet).
  5. “2. Excel ya CSV file chunein” → Files se wahi file chuno.
  6. Result dikhega: kitne order, kitne bill, kitne naye customer, kaunsi row samajh nahi aayi.

C. CSV file (agar kuch gadbad kare)

Excel → File → Save As → CSV UTF-8 → phone pe bhejo → wahi tarah import karein.

Header me kya ho (naam se pehchan hoti hai, order kuch bhi ho)

  • Sales: Date · Party · Product · Qty · Rate (ya Amount) · Paid date · Invoice no
  • Rates: Date · Supplier · Item · Rate · Freight · Credit days · Rejection %

App se Excel me wapas

Money → Orders + bills (CSV) ya Rate sheet (CSV) → file download → Excel / Google Sheets me kholein.

8. Routine

  • Roz (2 min): Today tab — jo kaam dikhe wo kar dein.
  • Hafte me (5 min): 2-3 supplier se naya rate, Rates me daalein.
  • Mahine ki 1 tareekh (10 min): Portfolio dekhein (laal circle = wahi kaam) aur Setup → Backup copy.

9. Teen baat saaf rakhni hai

  • Data sirf is phone me hai — backup ke bina phone gaya to data gaya.
  • App bazaar ka rate khud nahi banati; jo aap/supplier daalenge uska record aur faisla deti hai.
  • WhatsApp khud se message nahi bhej sakti — message taiyar hota hai, bhejne ka tap aap karte hain.

10. WhatsApp par bhejne ke liye chhota message

Sir, ye app aapke rate, paisa aur stock ka hisaab ek jagah rakhta hai. Isme har customer ka floor rate nikalta hai (lagat + byaaj + risk ke baad), payment reminder ka message banata hai, aur Portfolio me 6 circle me poora business dikhta hai. Link kholein → Share → Add to Home Screen. Pehle Setup me 8 number bharein, phir purana data Excel se import kar lein (Money → Excel). Data sirf aapke phone me rehta hai. 10 minute lagega, phir roz 2 minute.
